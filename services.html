01

Brand & GTM Strategy

The plan behind a launch, rebrand, or repositioning — built from 25 years of doing this at Schwab, LendingTree, Autodesk, and OneTrust. This is the work that saves money by making differentiation obvious before a single asset gets built.

  • Positioning & narrative development
  • Audience and channel strategy
  • Brand architecture for multi-product portfolios
  • Go-to-market planning
02

Narrative & Creative Direction

The story and voice that make a brand memorable — and the creative direction to keep it consistent from a keynote to a 15-second ad. This is where "Talk to Chuck" and LendingTree's Lenny came from.

  • Campaign concepting
  • Script and content direction
  • Creative review & sign-off

Advisory & Fractional Support

A senior brand and marketing exec in the room, without the full-time hire. Good fit for founders and marketing leaders who want a second set of eyes on a launch, rebrand, or campaign before it ships.

  • Marketing team sounding board
  • Pitch & board-deck review
